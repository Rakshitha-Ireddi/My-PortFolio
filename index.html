  • MVEB: Massive Video Embedding Benchmark

    arXiv · 2026

    A 23-task benchmark for video embeddings spanning classification, clustering, retrieval, and video-centric QA. Evaluated 33 models — no single architecture dominates, and audio's contribution depends on dataset annotation provenance. Integrates into the MTEB ecosystem for unified text/image/audio/video evaluation.

  • Through the Looking Glass: Benchmarking Mirror Image Reasoning in VLMs (MIRA)

    CVPR 2026 · MMRAgI Workshop

    Introduces MIRA, a benchmark for mirror-image and reflection reasoning in vision-language models. Across 11 state-of-the-art VLMs, uncovers a persistent limitation termed "reflection blindness" — models struggle with mirrored text, spatial orientation, chirality, and reflection-aware reasoning.
