Research

Research Website Google Scholar OpenReview

  • MVEB: Massive Video Embedding Benchmark

    EMNLP 2026

    A 23-task benchmark for video embeddings spanning classification, clustering, retrieval, and video-centric QA. Evaluated 33 models — no single architecture dominates, and audio's contribution depends on dataset annotation provenance. Integrates into the MTEB ecosystem for unified text/image/audio/video evaluation.

  • Through the Looking Glass: Benchmarking Mirror Image Reasoning in VLMs (MIRA)

    CVPR 2026 · MMRAgI Workshop

    Introduces MIRA, a benchmark for mirror-image and reflection reasoning in vision-language models. Across 11 state-of-the-art VLMs, uncovers a persistent limitation termed "reflection blindness" — models struggle with mirrored text, spatial orientation, chirality, and reflection-aware reasoning.

  • TRACE: Verifiable RL Environment for Network Troubleshooting Agents

    CMU · NeurIPS Workshop 2026 (submitted)

    A verifiable RL environment for autonomous network troubleshooting agents, enabling LLM agents to diagnose and resolve faults like routing and configuration drift.

  • APISHIFT: Verifiable Agent Learning for API Contract Migration

    CMU · NeurIPS Workshop 2026 (submitted)

    A verifiable agent-learning framework for API contract migration — detecting contract drift and auto-generating verified migration patches.

  • Can Large Language Models Autonomously Perform Decision Analysis? Where the Boundary Lies, on a Classical Influence Diagram

    Stanford University · Ongoing, 2026

    Tests seven models from four providers on the Oil Wildcatter influence diagram, where every answer is known exactly. The boundary lies not in arithmetic but in specification and verification: the model supplies judgment, code computes, and exact checks verify.

  • Resolving Spatial and Semantic Conflicts in Multi-LoRA Composition via Softmax Routing and Cross-Adapter Attention Conditioning

    CMU · Submitted, ICLR 2027

    Proposes two training-free mechanisms for multi-LoRA image composition: softmax-routed cross-attention masks that replace hard per-pixel routing, and cross-adapter attention conditioning that suppresses appearance leakage between overlapping concepts.

  • EdgeMedBench: Benchmarking Small Language Models for Medical QA
